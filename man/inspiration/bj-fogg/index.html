Who he is

The man who made habits tiny.

BJ Fogg founded the Behavior Design Lab at Stanford. He has spent decades studying what makes people act, and his answer is simpler than most: behaviour happens when three things meet at the same moment.

The core insight

B = MAP

Behaviour happens when Motivation, Ability and a Prompt meet. If one is missing, nothing happens. Motivation comes and goes, so Fogg works on the other two. Make the action so small it's easy on any day, and tie it to a prompt that already exists in your life.

His Tiny Habits method adds one more piece: celebrate straight after the action. A quick moment of feeling good helps the habit stick.

How MAN uses it

The daily rep.

MAN's daily rep is built on Fogg's model: "After I [cue], I say 3, 2, 1 and [tiny rep], then I [celebrate]."

Prompt: the cue
Something you already do every day, like pouring your coffee. MAN can add it to your calendar too.
Ability: the tiny rep
One small thing, the same every day, with a Partly version for flat days. It still counts.
Celebration
Two seconds after the rep. A nod, a "that's me". This part comes from Tiny Habits.

Fogg's model covers motivation, ability and the prompt, plus celebration. The rest of MAN's structure comes from Baz's 39 years of martial arts: the belt, the mates on the same path, and getting back up after a missed day. Those are MAN's additions, not part of Fogg's model.

Pillars
Action Mindset

Fogg's work sits mostly in Action, in how the rep actually happens. In MAN it follows Be: you picture the man you're becoming, and the tiny rep makes that choice real every day.
