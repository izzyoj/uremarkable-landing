⚖️ 4 Forces
Push
Life event creates genuine urgency. The lease ends in 90 days. Doing nothing now has a real cost.
Pull
Ownership equals stability. Fixed mortgage, owning something, being done searching.
Anxiety
"What if we buy too soon and prices fall?" / "What if we can't get finance?"
Habit
Still renting. Still browsing on Saturday morning without calling agents. "We're just looking."
Push
Portal browsing reveals the gap between expectation and reality. The house they imagined doesn't exist at their budget.
Pull
Off-market access is enormously attractive. An agent who knows what's coming before it hits portals.
Anxiety
Am I looking in the right suburb? Am I missing better value somewhere else?
Habit
Still doing all research independently. The ingrained assumption: agents only help when you've found a property.
Push
Portal enquiry is the lowest-friction action available. Momentum from the search phase carries them forward.
Pull
The property matches enough criteria to feel like it could be "the one." Enquiry is partly emotional validation.
Anxiety
Will the agent dismiss me because I haven't sold yet? Do I need to disclose conditional finance?
Habit
Sending enquiries and waiting. Not following up proactively to avoid seeming desperate.
Push
Portal search is exhausting. The inspection is relief from the endless scroll, a concrete in-person step forward.
Pull
The property looks better in person. Standing in the living room imagining their furniture. Emotional attachment starts here.
Anxiety
What am I not seeing? Is the strata body corporate hiding something? What will the building report say?
Habit
Inspecting multiple properties in parallel. Collecting impressions. Keeping emotional distance. "I need to see more."
Push
Real momentum from the inspection. They liked the property. The sunk cost of time and research is real.
Pull
The image of living in that property is specific now. The pull is resolution of a long, exhausting search.
Anxiety
Peak anxiety: a 60-page building report, a pending finance approval, and no agent in sight. Every unanswered question is a potential exit.
Habit
"If in doubt, don't." Australian household's default risk posture on major financial decisions is conservative.
Push
Search fatigue at peak. This property is the best match they've seen. The push to end the search is as strong as the push to buy right.
Pull
The life event is now pressing harder. The lease ending sooner. The job relocation date confirmed.
Anxiety
Am I about to make the most expensive mistake of my life? Am I going to miss it because someone else goes higher?
Habit
Underbidding first to "see where the vendor is." Using the first offer as an information-gathering move.
Push
Contracts are signed. No going back without financial penalty. The push is now legal commitment.
Pull
The property is theirs (almost). Planning the move, the renovation, the first night in the new house.
Anxiety
Finance not formally approved yet. Final inspection needed. The agent they trusted has mentally moved on.
Habit
Passively waiting for solicitor and broker to manage the process. Not reaching out because "that's done now."
💸 Where buyers drop off
  • No agent is present at this stage — the buyer hasn't engaged anyone yet
  • The agent who gets called first is usually the one on the portal listing the buyer happened to find, not the best-positioned one
  • No system capturing passive browsers as leads before first contact
  • Agents are absent during the buyer's research phase
  • The buyer forms opinions about suburbs, prices, and compromises entirely without agent input
  • When first contact happens, the agent has to undo as much as they reinforce
  • Average response time to portal enquiries: 4 to 18 hours
  • Many buyers book an inspection with a competing property before the reply arrives
  • The initial response, when it comes, is a generic auto-reply with no personalisation
  • The 90-day ready buyer gets the same response as the buyer 12 months away
  • Agents run 5 to 10 open homes on a Saturday morning — the buyer gets a minute or two of face time
  • Post-inspection, most agents send no follow-up specific to that buyer's experience
  • The buyer leaves with questions they didn't ask. The agent has no record of what mattered to them.
  • A significant share of seriously interested buyers exit at this stage — not because the property was wrong, but because the information gap was never closed
  • Agents treat this as the buyer's problem. "The B&P is their responsibility." Agents go quiet.
  • The buyer receives a 60-page report, panics at "major defect" language, calls the agent, gets vague reassurance, and buys somewhere else
  • Silence from the agent confirms they don't care whether the buyer proceeds
  • Most agents, under time pressure, give vague guidance on price range
  • Post-offer, "we'll get back to you" with no timeframe — buyer is in limbo
  • The feedback loop is too slow. Unable to pursue other properties, unable to close.
  • The post-exchange period is completely abandoned by most agents
  • The agent's economic incentive to stay engaged disappeared at exchange
  • Lost at two levels: the buyer never becomes a referral source and never becomes a future vendor lead

AI Automation Where AI closes the gap — keeping more buyers engaged from first signal through to offer
🤖 AI opportunities
Pre-Enquiry
  • AI-powered suburb guides and "what can you buy for $X in Y suburb" tools on the agent's website
  • Passive browsers converted to identified leads before first contact
  • Market condition summaries tailored to buyer's search area
Buyer Acquisition
  • AI buyer qualification tool captures criteria and returns a personalised suburb briefing
  • Positions the agent as a knowledgeable guide before the buyer has found a specific property
  • Off-market match alerts sent when suitable properties become available
Response Speed
  • Instant intelligent first response within 60 seconds: acknowledges the property, provides price guide, inspection times, and comparable sales, asks one qualifying question
  • Agent receives reply drafted and buyer qualification score before they see the notification
  • Highest-ROI AI intervention in the entire buyer journey
Inspection Follow-up
  • Post-inspection debrief within 2 hours: building report summary, strata levy detail, comparable sales table, recommended B&P inspector
  • AI sign-in app at the door captures buyer details, interest rating, and buying timeline automatically
  • Replaces clipboard-and-pen with structured buyer data for every open home
Due Diligence Support
  • B&P plain-language summary: buyer uploads report, AI surfaces the flagged items in plain language and generates the right questions to ask a qualified inspector — the buyer arrives at their follow-up call informed, not panicking
  • Proactive check-in at day 3 and day 7 post-inspection if no offer received: "Hey [name], I know you're working through the numbers. Can I answer anything?"
  • Finance confidence tool: confirms serviceability on this specific property at target price
Offer Guidance
  • Comparable sales dashboard in real time: 5 most comparable sales in 90 days with price-per-sqm and spread between guide and actual on this agent's last 10 campaigns
  • Private treaty: AI offer submission workflow with timestamps and automated buyer acknowledgement
  • Auction: AI-prepared comparable sales brief delivered 48 hours before auction day
Settlement & Retention
  • Automated settlement support sequence: week-by-week checklist after exchange — what to do, who to call, what documents are due
  • Post-settlement check-ins at 30 days, 90 days, and 12 months
  • Relationship pipeline that feeds referrals and future vendor listings automatically
