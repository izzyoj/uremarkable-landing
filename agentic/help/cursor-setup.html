Setting Up Cursor

Cursor is your free AI troubleshooting backup. When your assistant's usage window runs out, Cursor's built-in AI runs on its own quota independently. This guide covers installing it, opening the right folder, and getting AI help when something breaks.

Part 1 — Install Cursor
1
Download Cursor
Go to cursor.com and click the big download button. It will detect you're on a Mac automatically.
Free tier gives you 500 AI requests per month. That's plenty for troubleshooting a few times a week.
2
Install it
Open the downloaded file (it'll be in Downloads). Drag the Cursor icon into your Applications folder when prompted. Then open Cursor from Applications or Spotlight (Cmd+Space, type "Cursor").
3
Sign up for a free account
Cursor will ask you to sign in on first launch. Create a free account with your email. No credit card needed.
Part 2 — Open Your System Folder
4
Open your system folder in Cursor
In Cursor, go to the top menu: File > Open Folder

Navigate to your home folder (it usually opens there by default). You're looking for the folder your AI system runs from — it's called claudeclaw. Click it once to select it, then click Open.
Can't find it? In the folder picker, press Cmd+Shift+H to jump to your home folder. The claudeclaw folder should be right there.
5
Confirm you're in the right place
Once open, you'll see a list of files and folders on the left side panel. Things like dist/, scripts/, .env — that means you're in the right folder.
If you see something completely different or just a blank screen, close and try File > Open Folder again.
Part 3 — Open the Terminal Inside Cursor
6
Open a terminal panel
Go to the top menu: Terminal > New Terminal

A terminal panel will open at the bottom of the screen. It will automatically be pointed at your system folder. You're ready to type commands.
Shortcut: Ctrl + ` (backtick — the key above Tab)
7
Confirm you're in the right place
In the terminal, type this and press Enter:
pwd
It should say /Users/[yourname]/claudeclaw. If it does, you're in the right place.
Part 4 — Asking Cursor AI for Help
8
Open the AI chat
Press Cmd+L to open the Cursor AI chat panel on the right. This is where you ask for help when something breaks or you're not sure what to do.
9
What to say when something breaks
Just describe what happened in plain English. Examples that work well:
"My assistant stopped responding on Telegram. I'm in the claudeclaw folder. How do I restart it?"

"I got this error when I tried to restart: [paste the red text here]. What does it mean?"

"How do I check if the bot is currently running?"
Cursor AI can see the files in your folder. It will give you the exact commands to run, with explanations. Copy and paste them straight into the terminal panel below.

Still stuck?
If Cursor AI can't fix it, send support a Telegram with a screenshot of the red error text. Most issues can be diagnosed and resolved remotely in a few minutes.
