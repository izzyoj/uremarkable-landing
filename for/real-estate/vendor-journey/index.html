💸 Friction / leak
  • Every missed call = lost vendor or buyer lead
  • Slow first response drops conversion immediately
  • Unresponsiveness at the decision moment = gone for good
  • First 30 seconds of contact set the entire relationship tone
  • Rushed appraisal = vendor feels like a transaction
  • Weak or generic CMA = instant confidence loss
  • Poor welcome kit signals an amateur operation
  • Three agents are usually compared — the most prepared wins
  • Confusing fee structure = vendor price-shops to competitor
  • Unclear inclusions = pre-campaign anxiety begins
  • Paper-based process = friction at the commitment moment
  • Repeated FAQ calls = wasted agent time before campaign starts
  • Late or vague updates erode trust systematically
  • No visible progress = vendor calls to check in (agent time drain)
  • Marketing spend never clearly explained = feels unjustified
  • No milestone alerts = vendor always guessing what happens next
  • Delayed feedback = steady erosion of vendor confidence
  • Vague summaries = vendor questions agent engagement
  • No sentiment analysis = campaign not adapting to buyer signals
  • "Vague feel-good fluff" is the exact failure mode agents fall into
  • Pressure-led negotiation = vendor regret and blame post-sale
  • No comparative data = poor decision under stress
  • Missed conditions and risk flags = dispute exposure
  • Vendor makes emotional call without a framework to anchor to
  • Vendor confusion in contract is the single most common friction point
  • Reactive communication = trust breaks at the most critical stage
  • Any missed deadline here costs referrals and reputation
  • Vendors remember how this stage felt more than any other
  • Total silence after exchange = transactional impression locked in
  • No referral system = no compounding pipeline
  • Review capture left to chance = zero social proof accumulation
  • Property management opportunity missed entirely
AI Automation Where AI removes the manual drag and plugs the leaks — bucketed against the operational arm it affects
🤖 AI opportunities
Front Office / BDM
  • AI voice agent: 24/7 call answering, no missed leads
  • Seller-intent qualification on every enquiry
  • Auto-book appraisal from call or web form
  • Instant SMS response with booking confirmation
  • Suburb and reputation insight pack sent on booking
Sales
  • Automated CMA generation with comparable selection
  • Suburb micro-market commentary generated per property
  • AVM-supported price range pack ready before the meeting
  • Pre-appraisal vendor briefing sent via SMS
  • Agent-fit scoring to match vendor to right team member
Administration
  • Plain-English authority and fee explanation generated automatically
  • Clause-by-clause contract summary for vendor
  • E-sign listing agreement sent and tracked digitally
  • Automated FAQ response pack: fees, inclusions, timeline
  • Vendor dashboard activated on signature
Marketing / Sales
  • AI listing copywriter generates description from brief
  • Auto-push to REA, Domain, and social on schedule
  • Weekly vendor report auto-generated with KPIs
  • Campaign performance dashboard with buyer engagement data
  • Milestone alerts: listing live, first inspection, offer received
Sales
  • Open home check-in and attendee logging automation
  • Buyer follow-up SMS/email sent automatically post-inspection
  • Feedback sentiment analysis from buyer comments
  • Instant objection and concern summary delivered to vendor same day
  • Campaign adjustment recommendations based on buyer patterns
Sales Leadership
  • Offer comparison summaries in plain language
  • Risk flags on conditions: finance, building, settlement timing
  • Scenario analysis: price vs terms vs settlement risk
  • Buyer motivation scoring to inform negotiation approach
  • All negotiation comms logged and timestamped automatically
Administration
  • Milestone-triggered vendor status updates at each checkpoint
  • Task routing to conveyancers without manual follow-up
  • Deadline reminders and escalation alerts when a step is slipping
  • Proactive delay notification sent before anyone has to ask
  • All parties (vendor, buyer, conveyancer, finance) on one tracker
BDM / Property Mgmt
  • Automated thank-you message at settlement
  • Review request triggered at peak satisfaction (week 2)
  • Referral ask deployed at 90-day mark
  • Anniversary touchpoint and sold-price follow-up at 12 months
  • PM pipeline entry triggered automatically post-settlement
