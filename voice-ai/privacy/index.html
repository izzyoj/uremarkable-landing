Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 April 2026

This policy covers everyone who interacts with Scale Reception — including visitors to our website, prospective and current clients, people who contact us by phone, SMS, or email, and anyone who interacts with an AI voice agent or chatbot we have built or deployed. We are committed to handling your personal information with care, in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Plain-English Summary
  • We collect personal information when you visit our website, fill in a form, call us, or interact with an AI voice agent we've built.
  • We use your information to provide our services, respond to enquiries, and improve our AI systems — not to sell to third parties.
  • Calls handled by our AI agents may be recorded or transcribed. Where required, callers are notified before recording starts.
  • We use trusted third-party tools (telephony, AI platforms, scheduling, etc.) to deliver our service. Some of these operate outside Australia.
  • You can request access to, or correction of, your personal information at any time by contacting us.
Call Recording Notice (optional — for client deployments)

Calls to this service may be recorded or transcribed for the purposes of providing the service, ensuring quality, training AI systems, and generating notes or summaries for the business you are contacting. If recording applies to your call, you will be notified at the start of the conversation.

5 AI voice agents, chatbots, and call recordings

Our core service is building and deploying AI voice agents that answer phone calls on behalf of businesses. This section explains how we handle information in that context.

AI disclosure and how to know you're talking to an AI

When you call a number powered by our AI voice agent, we disclose that you are speaking with an artificial intelligence system. This disclosure typically occurs at the start of the call or in a greeting message. We believe in being transparent about AI involvement so callers understand the nature of the interaction.

Call recordings and transcripts

Calls handled by an AI voice agent we have deployed may be recorded and/or automatically transcribed. We may retain these recordings and transcripts to:

  • Deliver the service (for example, generating call summaries, forwarding booking requests, or creating notes)
  • Monitor call quality and performance
  • Identify errors or system improvements
  • Train and improve AI system accuracy and responses
  • Comply with legal or business requirements

Recording notice and consent

Where a call is being recorded, we take reasonable steps to notify the caller before recording begins — typically by playing a disclosure message at the start of the call. The message informs the caller that the call may be recorded for service, quality, training, or note-taking purposes.

Callers are generally free to end the call if they do not consent to recording. If a caller continues after the notification, their continuation is treated as consent to recording.

Data collected from calls

During a call to an AI agent we have deployed, the following information may be collected:

  • Audio recording: the full call or call segments
  • Transcripts: text conversion of spoken words
  • Phone number: the caller's phone number (may be captured by telephony systems)
  • Call metadata: call duration, date, time, and any call routing information
  • Interaction content: details provided by the caller (e.g. name, query type, booking details)

State and territory recording laws

Call recording laws vary significantly across Australian states and territories. Some key points:

  • Some states require all-party consent: In jurisdictions like New South Wales and Victoria, all parties to a conversation must consent before a call can be recorded.
  • Some states apply one-party consent: In other jurisdictions, one party may be able to record without all parties' knowledge (though this is becoming less common).
  • Business context: When a business receives an incoming call, there may be different consent rules compared to outgoing calls.

We work with our clients to configure systems that comply with the laws of their state or territory. If you have questions about whether a specific call was recorded or about the applicable recording laws, you may contact the business whose AI agent you interacted with, or contact us directly.

AI model processing

AI voice agents we deploy may use third-party AI models to understand and respond to callers. This processing occurs via platforms we integrate with, such as voice agent infrastructure and large language model providers. Input to these models may include spoken words converted to text. We choose providers that operate appropriate data handling standards and we apply contractual protections where possible.

No autonomous decision-making

Our AI agents are designed to handle routine enquiries and bookings. They do not make significant decisions about individuals (such as eligibility decisions, approvals, or rejections) without human involvement. If an AI agent cannot assist or detects a sensitive matter, it will typically offer to connect the caller with a human or take a message for follow-up.

7 Our tools

Scale Reception uses the following key platforms and tools to build and deliver AI voice receptionist services:

Data processed by these tools may be transferred to, stored on, or processed on servers located outside Australia, including in the United States. We have data processing agreements in place with these providers where possible to ensure they handle personal information appropriately. However, we cannot guarantee the same level of privacy protection as Australian law may provide, as some overseas jurisdictions have different privacy standards.

10 Accuracy and limitations of AI

While we work hard to provide accurate AI voice agents, artificial intelligence systems are not perfect and may make mistakes. Callers and clients should be aware of the following:

AI can make errors

Our AI agents may occasionally misunderstand caller requests, provide inaccurate information, or make inappropriate responses. We continuously work to improve accuracy, but perfection is not achievable. If you notice an error or inappropriate response from an AI agent, we encourage you to report it to the business you contacted.

Human review of important information

For important matters — such as property details, pricing, availability, or service terms — we recommend that information provided by an AI agent be verified with a human representative. We design our agents to flag sensitive or important information for human follow-up where appropriate.

Limitations and responsible use

Our AI agents are designed to handle routine enquiries and bookings. They should not be relied upon for:

  • Complex legal or financial advice
  • Critical decisions that affect an individual's rights or safety
  • Handling of sensitive personal or health information without clear consent
  • Services requiring qualified professional judgment

When an AI agent encounters a matter outside its scope, it should escalate to a human or decline to proceed.

11 Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as it is needed to provide our services and meet our legal or contractual obligations. After that, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or de-identify the information.

For call recordings and transcripts generated by AI agents deployed for clients, we typically retain these recordings for 90 days from the date of the call. This retention period allows us to review calls for service improvement, identify system errors, and refine our AI agent responses. After 90 days, recordings are securely deleted unless a longer retention period is required by the relevant client or applicable law.
