The point

Built on shoulders, not handed down from them.

These aren't endorsements and they're not a reading list. Each of these thinkers contributed a specific mechanism to how MAN works. The protocol borrows the insight, translates it into a daily system, and applies it to a specific problem: closing the gap between who you are and who you're becoming.

None of them built MAN. But none of MAN would exist without them.
