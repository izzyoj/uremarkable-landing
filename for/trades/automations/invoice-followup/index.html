Setup walkthrough

How it's built in Cowork

Five steps. One session. You leave with a running system that chases invoices while you're on the tools.

0
Create your Cowork project
Create a dedicated Cowork project for invoice follow-up. Name it clearly (e.g. "Peak Plumbing: Invoice Follow-Up"). This project keeps its own memory and instructions, separate from anything else you run in Cowork.
New project • scoped to your business
1
Install the plugin
Open Cowork, click Customize in the left sidebar, then Browse plugins. Upload the Invoice Follow-Up plugin file. One upload installs everything: the Xero connector, message templates, Gmail or Outlook connector, and Twilio integration.
Cowork Plugins • one upload
2
Set your business voice
Go to Settings > Cowork > Global Instructions. Tell Claude your name, your business name, and how you like to communicate with clients. The plugin uses this to write follow-ups in your voice, not a template voice. Add your bank details here too so they appear in the Day 7 email automatically.
Settings → Cowork → Global Instructions
3
Connect Xero
Authorise the Xero MCP connector in Cowork. The plugin watches your invoice list and monitors payment status. When an invoice flips from Due to Overdue, it fires the sequence. It also watches for Paid status and cancels any pending messages the moment payment clears.
Xero MCP → authorise in one step
4
Connect email and Twilio
Day 3 and Day 14 messages send as SMS from your business Twilio number. Day 7 sends as email from your Gmail or Outlook. Authorise both connectors in the same session. Takes about 10 minutes for Twilio setup if you don't have an account yet. Included in your setup session.
Gmail or Outlook MCP • Twilio MCP
5
Set the trigger and activate Dispatch
Configure the overdue trigger in Cowork using the trigger config inside the plugin. Link Dispatch so you get a notification on your phone before each message sends. One tap approves it, or let it run fully automated if you prefer hands-off.
Trigger on overdue • Dispatch linked • live
What to know before you set up

Honest limitations

This is real infrastructure. Here's exactly what it requires and where the edges are.

💻
Desktop must stay on. Scheduled messages only fire while your machine is awake and Claude Desktop is running. If it sleeps between Day 3 and Day 7, the next message sends when it wakes. For critical invoices, keep the machine on or use the Dispatch approval flow so you can monitor from your phone. Source: Anthropic: Schedule recurring tasks in Claude Cowork
💬
SMS sends from a Twilio number, not your mobile. Clients receive texts from your dedicated business number, not your personal phone. That's intentional, but it does require a Twilio account. Setup is about 10 minutes and is included in your onboarding session. Twilio setup: one-off, included in the setup session
💲
Xero native. MYOB via custom setup. Xero connects in one step via its native MCP connector. MYOB AccountRight is supported via a custom MCP build. More setup, but fully functional. Ask about MYOB pricing at your session. Xero: native MCP  ·  MYOB: custom engagement
▶
Dispatch requires both Desktop and mobile apps. The approval notification goes to your mobile via the Cowork Dispatch app. The actual send happens on your desktop. Both need to be installed. Available on Pro and Max plans only. Source: Anthropic: Assign tasks from anywhere in Claude Cowork
