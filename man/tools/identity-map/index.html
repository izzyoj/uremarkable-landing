Identity Map

Three steps. Ten minutes. The gap between who you say you are and who you're actually being. Made visible.

Step 1 of 3

Who do you say you are?

Write three statements about the kind of person you are. No filter. The identity you declare. To yourself, to others.

Step 2 of 3

What does your behaviour actually show?

Honest. Not what you intended. What you did. Look at the last two weeks. What patterns does someone watching you actually see?

Step 3 of 3

What votes will you cast today?

Three specific actions: small, doable today. Actions the person you declared yourself to be would take. Not goals. Votes.
