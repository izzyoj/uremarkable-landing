Getting started
What exactly is the AI Business Operating System? +

Your AI Business Operating System is the layer that connects Claude (Anthropic's AI) to your Telegram. When Baz sets it up on your Mac, every message you send in Telegram goes directly to Claude running on your own machine. The reply comes back to your phone within seconds.

It's not a chatbot or a cloud service. Claude runs locally on your hardware, reads your files, connects to your apps, and operates like a member of your team you can reach at any hour.

What do I need before my setup session with Baz? +

Three things:

  • A Mac (MacOS is the primary setup; Windows is supported but less common)
  • A Telegram account, any existing one works
  • A Claude plan. Free and Pro work, but Claude Max (~$155/month AUD) on Opus is the recommended experience for anything complex

Baz handles everything else: software installation, connecting your apps, configuring the assistant to know about your business.

How long does setup take? +

The software configuration itself takes about 5 minutes. The full setup session with Baz typically runs 60-90 minutes. This covers installing everything, connecting your apps (email, calendar, WhatsApp), and personalising the assistant so it knows your business, your preferences, and what you'll use it for most.

Does it work on Windows? +

Yes, through WSL2, a compatibility layer that lets Windows run Linux apps. Baz's standard setup is Mac-first, but Windows is supported. If you're on Windows, mention it before booking your session.

Which Claude plan do I need? +

Any plan works. Free, Pro, or Max. The difference is in performance on complex tasks:

  • Max (~$155/month AUD): recommended. Opus model handles multi-step tasks, building tools, and anything requiring real reasoning. Best overall experience.
  • Pro (~$31/month AUD): works for day-to-day messages, but may struggle on complex or long-running tasks.
  • Free: limited but functional for simple queries.
Baz's setup is optimised for Max. If you're on Pro and hit limits, upgrading is the fastest fix.
What is a "Telegram bot" and why do I need one? +

Telegram allows you to create a private bot, basically a dedicated chat account that belongs to you. Your AI Business Operating System lives inside this bot. You message it like any contact, and it responds.

Baz creates this during your setup session using Telegram's BotFather tool. You end up with a private Telegram chat connected exclusively to your machine. No one else can access it.

Does it need to be running all the time? What happens when I close my laptop? +

Your AI Business Operating System runs as a background service on your Mac. It starts automatically when you log in and restarts itself if it ever crashes. You don't need to open a terminal or do anything manually.

If your Mac is closed or shut down, the assistant won't respond until it's back on. For scheduled tasks (like a morning briefing), your Mac needs to be running at that time.

Day to day use
How do I actually use it? Just type? +

Yes. Open the Telegram chat Baz set up and type anything. No wake word, no special format. Just describe what you want and send it.

Some examples that work out of the box:

  • "Check my calendar for tomorrow"
  • "Draft a reply to the email from Sarah about the proposal"
  • "What's the most recent message in my WhatsApp from James?"
  • "Create a summary of this document and send it to me as a PDF"
  • "Set a reminder to follow up with the Barangaroo lead on Friday"
Can I use voice notes instead of typing? +

Yes. Hold the microphone button in Telegram, record, and send. It transcribes automatically and executes your request, same as if you'd typed it.

By default, replies come back as text. To get a voice reply back, say one of these anywhere in your message:

  • "respond with voice"
  • "send me a voice back"
  • "voice reply"

To switch voice replies on for every message, send /voice. Send it again to turn it off.

Can I send photos, documents, and PDFs? +

All of these work. Just attach the file and send, with or without a caption:

  • Photos: analyzed immediately. No caption needed, but add one for specific instructions: "what are the key numbers in this?"
  • PDFs, Word docs, CSVs, text files: read and processed. Try "summarize this", "extract the dates and amounts", or "draft a reply based on this"
  • Videos: analyzed up to 20MB. Add a caption for what you need from it.
Can it create files and send them back to me? +

Yes. Ask it to create something and it'll send the file as a Telegram attachment. Works up to 50MB.

Examples:

  • "Summarize this week's emails into a PDF and send it to me"
  • "Build me a one-page comparison of these two quotes and send it as a spreadsheet"
  • "Create an HTML proposal for [client name] and send it"
What commands can I use? +

Type / in Telegram to see the full list. The most useful ones:

  • /newchat: start a fresh conversation (clears current context)
  • /respin: send immediately after /newchat to pull recent messages back in
  • /voice: toggle voice replies on/off for all messages
  • /model haiku / /model sonnet / /model opus: switch between speed and power
  • /memory: see what your assistant remembers about you
  • /stop: cancel whatever it's doing mid-task
  • /dashboard: get a link to your live dashboard
  • /wa: open WhatsApp interface
  • /slack: open Slack interface
How do I start a fresh conversation? +

Send /newchat. This wipes the current session and starts completely fresh.

If you want to keep your recent messages for context, send /respin immediately after. It pulls the last 20 messages into the new session so the assistant knows what you were just discussing, without carrying the full weight of a long conversation.

If responses start feeling off or slower than usual, /newchat + /respin is the fix 90% of the time.
What is the dashboard? +

A live web page that shows everything happening inside your assistant: scheduled tasks, what it remembers about you, how much you're spending on API calls, and whether everything is running healthy.

Send /dashboard in Telegram to get a clickable link. If Baz set up remote access during your session, the link works from your phone anywhere.

Memory
Does it remember things between conversations? +

Yes. Your AI Business Operating System has two memory layers working together:

  • Within a session: full context of everything said in the current conversation
  • Short-term (conversation memory): recent back-and-forth is stored locally and surfaces automatically when relevant. Auto-purges after 3 days.
  • Long-term (second brain): important things, preferences, decisions, and business context get written to Obsidian, a local note-taking app on your Mac. This layer never fades.

You don't need to re-explain who you are or what you're working on every time you open Telegram.

What is the second brain and how is it different from regular memory? +

The two memory layers do completely different jobs:

  • Conversation memory (SQLite): short-term, auto-purges after 3 days. Holds the recent back-and-forth and surfaces context at the start of each session.
  • Second brain (Obsidian): permanent. Everything that should stick around lives here. Notes you've captured, daily logs, research, tools built for your business, project records, decisions. This does not fade.

Obsidian is a free note-taking app. Your vault is a folder on your Mac with a standard structure the assistant writes to automatically:

YourBrain/
├── inbox/         # quick captures, voice note transcripts
├── daily/         # daily notes, YYYY-MM-DD.md
├── projects/      # active work, decisions, research
├── content/       # content pipeline
│   └── linkedin/
│       ├── queue/ # drafts ready to post
│       └── posted/# confirmed published
├── tools/         # HTML tools, dashboards, proposals built for you
├── assets/        # reusable contact details, credentials
└── memory.md      # readable log of what your assistant knows

Everything in the vault is plain text files. Readable in any editor, not locked to any platform or subscription. The graph view in Obsidian shows every note and how they connect, a visual map of everything that's been built for your business.

Do I need to set up or manage Obsidian myself? +

Baz installs it and connects it to your assistant during your setup session. You don't need to configure anything.

After setup, your assistant writes to the vault automatically:

  • Things you capture in conversation land in inbox/
  • Daily notes get written at the start of each day (if you have that scheduled)
  • Tools and files Baz builds for you go in tools/
  • Research and project notes go in projects/

You can open Obsidian at any time to browse, search, or edit what's accumulated. Most people end up using it as a live record of everything their business has built and decided, without ever having to maintain it manually.

Obsidian is free. Download it at obsidian.md before your setup session if you want a head start.
How long does it remember things for? +

It depends on how important the memory is and how often it gets used:

  • Core preferences and key rules last about 15 months (1% decay per day)
  • General business context and decisions last about 8 months (2% decay per day)
  • Frequently referenced memories last longer. Each time a memory surfaces in a conversation it gets a salience boost

Memories that prove useless fade out. Memories that keep coming up stay longer. The system learns what matters over time.

How do I see what it remembers about me? +

Send /memory. You'll see the recent memories stored for your chat.

You can also browse the full memory landscape in the dashboard. It shows importance scores, what's recently been retrieved, and anything that's starting to fade.

Can I make it forget something? +

Send /forget to clear the current session ID. This doesn't delete stored memories. Those decay naturally over time.

If there's something specific you want removed, tell your assistant directly: "forget what I told you about [X]" and it will mark that memory as low importance so it fades faster.

Connecting your apps
Can it read and send emails? +

Yes, if Gmail was connected during your setup session. Ask it to:

  • "Check my inbox and tell me what needs attention"
  • "Reply to [name]'s email saying I'll confirm by Friday"
  • "Send an email to [address] about [topic]"
  • "Find all unread emails from [company]"

It uses Google's official OAuth. Your password never touches the system and it won't bypass your 2FA.

Can it manage my calendar? +

Yes, if Google Calendar was connected during setup. Ask it to:

  • "What's on my calendar this week?"
  • "Schedule a call with [name] on Thursday at 2pm"
  • "Send [name] a calendar invite for Friday morning"
  • "Am I free next Monday between 10am and 12pm?"
Can it use WhatsApp? +

Yes, with no extra cost or API key. It connects to your existing WhatsApp account via Linked Devices, the same way WhatsApp Web works on your laptop. You scan a QR code once during setup, and it stays connected.

Send /wa to see your most recent chats (unread first). Select one, read the messages, and reply, all from Telegram. You also get a notification when someone messages you on WhatsApp.

Your WhatsApp message content is encrypted at rest and auto-deleted after 3 days. It never leaves your Mac.
Can it use Slack? +

Yes, if Slack was configured during setup. Send /slack to see recent conversations with unread counts. Select a conversation, read it, and reply.

You can also ask naturally: "check my Slack" or "reply to Jane on Slack saying I'll be 10 minutes late".

Can it access Google Drive, Sheets, or Docs? +

This requires the Google Workspace CLI, an optional tool Baz can install during or after your session. Once configured, your assistant can read, write, and search across Drive, Sheets, Docs, and every other Workspace API.

If you need this, mention it to Baz and it can be added in a follow-up session.

Automation and scheduling
Can I set it up to do things automatically? +

Yes. Just tell it in plain language what you want and when:

  • "Every weekday morning at 8am, check my inbox and send me a briefing"
  • "Every Monday at 9am, pull last week's AI news and summarize it"
  • "Every Friday at 5pm, tell me what's on for next week"

It creates and manages the schedule itself. Your Mac needs to be running at the scheduled time.

How do I manage scheduled tasks? +

Just ask in plain language:

  • "What tasks do I have scheduled?"
  • "Pause the Monday morning briefing"
  • "Delete the daily email check"
  • "Change the morning briefing to 7:30am instead of 8am"

You can also view and manage them directly on the dashboard under Scheduled Tasks.

What are "agents" and do I need more than one? +

Agents are specialist versions of your assistant, each with their own Telegram bot and focused role. Examples: a Comms agent that handles email and WhatsApp, a Research agent for deep lookups, an Ops agent for admin tasks.

Each agent gets its own 1-million-token context window, its own personality, and its own scheduled tasks. They share your data and skills.

Most users start with a single main assistant and add agents later as workload grows. It's entirely optional.

Security and privacy
Who else can access my bot? +

Only you. The system locks to your specific Telegram account using your chat ID. Messages from any other Telegram user are silently dropped. They won't get a response or even an error. Group chats are blocked entirely.

You can also add a PIN lock for extra security. When enabled, the bot ignores all messages until you send the correct PIN. There's also an emergency kill phrase that immediately stops all processes if you ever need a hard shutdown.

Does my data leave my machine? +

Your data stays on your Mac. Conversations, files, memories, all stored locally in a database on your machine.

The external calls that do happen:

  • Anthropic: receives your individual queries to run Claude. Does not receive your full conversation history or stored memories.
  • Google: if you connected Gmail, Calendar, or video analysis. Uses standard Google OAuth.
  • Groq: if voice input is enabled, your voice note is transcribed there. Free tier.
Are my WhatsApp and Slack messages stored safely? +

Yes. Message content is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM before being written to your local database. If someone accessed the database file directly, they'd see ciphertext, not your messages.

Messages are also automatically deleted after 3 days. This is enforced in the code and can't be disabled without modifying the source.

Is this against Anthropic's rules? +

No. Your AI Business Operating System runs the official claude CLI on your machine. It doesn't extract OAuth tokens, impersonate Anthropic's infrastructure, or make API calls outside the official SDK.

What it does is identical to typing claude in your own terminal. The input comes from Telegram and the output goes back there. Anthropic's terms allow exactly this kind of personal use.

Troubleshooting
My bot isn't responding +

A few things to check in order:

  • Is your Mac on and awake? The assistant only works when the machine is running.
  • macOS permission dialogs. On first run, your Mac shows "Node wants to access..." dialogs. If you missed clicking Allow, the bot hangs silently. Check your Mac screen.
  • Are you messaging the right bot? It only responds to the Telegram account Baz linked during setup.
  • Service check. Contact Baz and ask him to run npm run status remotely to check if everything is healthy.
The bot is responding but feels confused or off +

The conversation context window is probably getting too heavy after a long session. Fix:

  • Send /newchat
  • Immediately send /respin

This wipes the full session but pulls your recent messages back in, giving you fresh context without losing the thread of what you were doing.

Voice notes aren't working +

Voice transcription requires a Groq API key. If this wasn't configured during your setup session, contact Baz. The Groq free tier is more than enough for personal use. No credit card required.

If voice replies (audio back to you) aren't working, that's a separate thing. It requires an ElevenLabs API key and a voice clone. Mention to Baz which part isn't working.

WhatsApp isn't connecting or showing my messages +

WhatsApp requires a separate background process running alongside your AI Business Operating System. If it's stopped, you'll need to restart it and re-scan the QR code in WhatsApp on your phone.

The QR code expires after about 30 seconds. If it times out, kill the process and restart it. Contact Baz if you're unsure how to do this.

How do I update to the latest version? +

Ask your assistant directly: "update the AI Business Operating System to the latest version" and it will walk you through the process on your machine.

Or contact Baz. Updates are part of the ongoing maintenance included in your retainer.

I got an error about a "409 Conflict" +

This means the system is running twice, with two processes both trying to receive Telegram messages. Restart your Mac, or contact Baz to clear the duplicate process. It's a quick fix.
