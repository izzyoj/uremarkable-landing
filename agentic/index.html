Common questions

What people ask
before booking.

What is Claude Cowork and how is it different from Claude.ai? +
Claude.ai is the browser interface. You chat with it, it answers, and it forgets you next session. Claude Cowork is Anthropic's desktop product built for real ongoing work. It stays connected to your context, integrates with your local files, and Uremarkable builds a complete operating system on top of it, with your clients, your workflows, and your rules loaded from day one.
What's a CLAUDE.md and why does it matter? +
CLAUDE.md is the file that tells your AI who you are. Your clients, your tone, your common workflows, your business rules, written once and loaded into every session. Instead of re-explaining yourself every time you open the app, it already knows. The difference between a tool and a hire is memory. CLAUDE.md is the memory.
Do I need a developer or any technical skills? +
No. Baz handles the technical build async before your setup session. You don't need to be present while it's being built. You show up to a 60-minute Zoom and leave with your first workflow running. Day to day, it's just talking to it.
